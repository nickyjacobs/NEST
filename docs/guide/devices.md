# Your notes on a phone or a second computer

← [Docs](../README.md)

**On this page:** [What you get](#what-you-get) · [Which route](#which-route) · [What it asks of you](#what-it-asks-of-you) · [What is not offered](#what-is-not-offered)

Your wiki is a folder of notes on the computer where you built your workspace. The setup asks
where else you want to read and write them, and proposes a way to keep them in step. You can
say "later": the wiki works without it.

## What you get

- Your notes on every device you choose, the same everywhere.
- A note you make on your phone lands in your inbox. Your assistant files it the next time you
  sit down at your computer.
- Your start page, colours and buttons on the other device too, after one extra step there.

What stays on the first computer: the assistant itself, its routines and the guards. A phone
shows and edits your notes; it does not run your assistant. That is on the
[roadmap](../../ROADMAP.md).

## Which route

| You have | The setup proposes | Free alternative |
|---|---|---|
| An iPhone or iPad | Obsidian Sync | Syncthing with an iOS app, for the technically minded |
| An Android phone | Obsidian Sync | Syncthing on both devices |
| A second computer, for your notes | Obsidian Sync | Syncthing on both computers |
| A work laptop where nothing may be installed | Obsidian Sync, if Obsidian is allowed there | None |

**Obsidian Sync** is Obsidian's own service. It is the one route someone new to all this can
set up alone, and it costs money: about 4 dollars a month when paid per year, in November 2026.
Check the [current price](https://obsidian.md/pricing) before you decide.

**Syncthing** is a free program that keeps a folder the same on two devices, directly between
them. No account and no cost. You install it on both, pair them, and choose the folder on each.

The setup gives you the steps for your route one at a time. They are also in
[the kit](../../kit/wiki/obsidian/DEVICES.md), which is what your assistant reads.

## What it asks of you

- An account, a payment or an app on your phone is yours to arrange. The setup never signs in
  for you and never sees a password.
- On every device Obsidian asks once whether you trust the author of the vault. Say yes, then
  close Obsidian and open it again, or your buttons show as text.
- With Obsidian Sync, switch on the syncing of appearance and plugins in its settings, on each
  device. They are off by default, and without them the other device shows a bare vault.
- Work notes in a personal sync account: check your employer's rules first.

## What is not offered

- **Your workspace in iCloud Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox or Google Drive.** Those services want
  the notes inside their own folder, and a workspace must not live in a synced folder: sync and
  git damage each other's files.
- **Git on your phone.** It works for people who use git every day, and nobody else.
- **Your whole workspace on a second computer,** assistant and all. That is on the roadmap.

None of the routes on this page has been tried on a real phone by the makers of NEST yet. The
steps come from Obsidian's own documentation. Tell us in
[Discussions](https://github.com/nickyjacobs/NEST/discussions) how it went.
