# Code of conduct

NEST is a small project. These few lines are how we treat each other in issues, pull requests
and discussions.

- **Be kind and specific.** Say what you saw, what you expected and what would help. Criticise
  the idea or the code, never the person.
- **Assume good intent, and make yours easy to see.** People here have different languages,
  levels and setups. A beginner's question is as welcome as an expert's patch.
- **No harassment.** No insults, no personal attacks, no sexual language or imagery, no
  publishing of someone's private information, no pressure after a no.
- **Keep private things private.** Never post secrets, client data or details about other
  people, also not in an example or a log.

## When something is wrong

Report it to the maintainer in private, through
[a private report on GitHub](https://github.com/nickyjacobs/NEST/security/advisories/new). Say
what happened and where. Reports are read by the maintainer only and handled with discretion.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads and block accounts that do not follow
this code.
