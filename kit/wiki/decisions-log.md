# Decisions log

Every real decision: a choice between alternatives, with the reason. Newest first, right under the format block. An entry is never rewritten; a decision that replaces an older one says so, and the old entry gets a `**Status:** superseded by ...` line.

## Format

```markdown
## YYYY-MM-DD: <short title>

- **Area:** <one of the areas below>
- **Decision:** what was decided, one or two sentences
- **Rationale:** why, and what was the alternative
- **Impact:** what changes because of it
- **Reference:** a link to the session, project or idea page it came from
```

Areas: <fill in: the user's areas, for example workspace, wiki, study, career, home, money>
